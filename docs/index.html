Goal Visualizer

Free, privacy-first running analytics for Strava

What is Goal Visualizer?

A lightweight analytics dashboard that helps runners track progress toward annual goals using Strava data. Connect your account to view personal progress, club leaderboards, and interactive charts—all with complete privacy and control.
