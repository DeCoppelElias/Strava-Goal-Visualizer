Delete Data from the App

  1. Open the dashboard app and go to Privacy Settings.
  2. Verify your identity with Strava.
  3. Click Disconnect and Delete Everything.
  4. Confirm completion message in the dashboard.

What Is Deleted

  • Local user/account records in this app.
  • Stored OAuth token records for the connected account.
  • Locally synced activity records associated with your account.

Strava Authorization

The app attempts to revoke Strava authorization during disconnect. You can also revoke access directly in your Strava account settings.
